# Contributing

To maintain consistency and preserve the author's editorial direction, **this project does not accept external contributions via pull requests**.

If you find typos, incorrect explanations, or inaccurate information, please report them through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/qu3vipon/the-hitchhikers-guide-to-ai/issues).

Please include a link to the affected page and details of the issue. Reports will be reviewed, and corrections will be made as appropriate.

Thank you for reading and for your interest in this project.

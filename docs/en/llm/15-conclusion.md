# Conclusion {#conclusion}

We have traveled from the foundations of machine learning through neural networks and transformers to the principles behind LLMs.

The guide follows how ideas emerged and developed, connecting each topic to the next. Diagrams and accessible explanations provide the main thread, while detailed mathematics and implementation are kept in the background.

This is not an exhaustive account. We have omitted or only briefly discussed many important topics: other foundational models, reinforcement learning, convolutional networks, AlexNet, and much more.

The goal is to provide a **big picture and a working set of concepts**, not explain everything about AI. I hope these ideas help you connect unfamiliar material when you read research papers, technical documentation, or more advanced books.

Your interest and support help make better learning material possible. I hope this guide has given you a useful starting point for understanding AI—and the confidence to keep exploring.
